ABOUT OUR HOSPITAL

Healthcare with dignity and compassion

Kailash Hospital, located near Anaj Mandi, Balaji Colony, Farrukhnagar, Gurugram, Haryana – 122506, is committed to providing accessible, affordable and compassionate healthcare services to the community.

Our mission is to make quality medical care available to people from all sections of society, with special attention to patients who need affordable treatment and medical support. We believe that healthcare is not just about treating illness; it is also about caring for people with dignity, respect and kindness.

Kailash Hospital offers a range of healthcare services, including General OPD, IPD, Operation Theatre, Laboratory, X-Ray and Pharmacy facilities. Patients can also seek medical consultation in specialties such as Gynecology, Orthopedics and Skin Care.

The hospital is led by Dr. Manish Kumar Pandey, Director, and Dr. Madhu Bala Pandey, Managing Director, along with a team of medical professionals dedicated to patient care.

Through our association with Pandit Dina Nath Charitable Trust, we aim to support charitable healthcare initiatives and help patients in need. We also welcome contributions from individuals and organizations who wish to support our mission of serving humanity.

Our website allows visitors to explore hospital services, learn about our doctors, request appointments through WhatsApp and contribute towards charitable patient care through the available donation facility.
